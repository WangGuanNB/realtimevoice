# Privacy Policy — RealtimeVoice

**Effective Date: May 9, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to **RealtimeVoice** ("we," "us," or "our"). RealtimeVoice is a production platform for building and deploying voice agents powered by GPT Realtime 2 — OpenAI's latest speech-to-speech model. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using our service at [realtimevoice.app](https://realtimevoice.app).

By using RealtimeVoice, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information

- **What We Collect**: Your name, email address, and authentication credentials when you create an account.
- **Purpose**: To manage your account, deliver customer support, send transactional emails (invoices, alerts, verification), and provide account-related features.

### 2. Voice Agent Configuration Data

- **What We Collect**: System prompts, agent settings, reasoning effort levels, voice selections, tool schemas, and deployment configurations you create on our platform.
- **Purpose**: To store and serve your agent configurations so they are available across sessions and channels.

### 3. Session and Call Logs

- **What We Collect**: Conversation transcripts, tool call records, session duration, latency metrics, and per-session cost breakdowns from voice agent sessions run through your account.
- **Purpose**: To power call analytics, support cost tracking dashboards, and help you monitor and improve your agents' performance.
- **Important**: Voice audio is processed through OpenAI's API using **your own API key**. RealtimeVoice does not intercept, record, or store raw audio streams. Audio flows directly between your deployment channel and OpenAI.

### 4. Usage and Device Data

- **What We Collect**: Platform interactions (features used, settings changed), aggregated session data, browser type, device type, operating system, and basic technical diagnostics.
- **Purpose**: To ensure compatibility, debug issues, understand feature usage, and guide product improvements.

### 5. Cookies and Local Storage

- **What We Store**: Session state, preferences (theme, locale), and anonymous analytics identifiers.
- **Purpose**: To maintain a consistent, fast experience, remember your choices, and analyze anonymized usage trends.

### 6. Billing and Payment Information

- **What We Collect**: Payment method details, billing address, and transaction metadata when you purchase a paid plan.
- **Purpose**: To process transactions, manage subscription entitlements, and comply with financial regulations.
- Payments are handled by trusted third-party providers (Stripe, PayPal, Creem). We do not store full payment card details on our servers.

## Your OpenAI API Key

RealtimeVoice requires you to connect your own OpenAI API key. Your key is encrypted at rest and in transit. It is used exclusively to operate the voice agents you configure — we never use your key for any other purpose and never share it with third parties. All OpenAI API usage and associated costs remain under your own OpenAI account.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide, operate, and improve the RealtimeVoice platform and its features.
- To display real-time cost tracking, session analytics, and billing summaries in your dashboard.
- To send transactional and service-related communications (account verification, invoices, usage alerts).
- To detect, investigate, and prevent security incidents and abuse.
- To comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your information.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data:

- **Service Providers**: With vetted vendors who assist in operating the service (cloud infrastructure, analytics, payment processors), bound by confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or valid legal processes.
- **Safety and Protection**: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or RealtimeVoice.
- **Aggregated Statistics**: We may share anonymized, aggregated data that does not identify any individual user.

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at [support@realtimevoice.app](mailto:support@realtimevoice.app).

## Your Choices and Rights

- **Access and Correction**: You may request access to or correction of your personal information at any time.
- **Deletion**: You may request deletion of your account and personal data.
- **Data Export**: You may export your agent configurations and session logs through your account settings.
- **Cookie Preferences**: You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.
- **Analytics Opt-Out**: Where available, you may opt out of non-essential analytics.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA). Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Children's Privacy

RealtimeVoice is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action to remove that information.

## Changes to This Policy

Your continued use of the service after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. For material changes, we will notify you by email or through a prominent notice on the platform.
